Bank PHY2-DC-SPRTC-10222026 · created 10-29-2026 · 10 problems

Isomorphic numerical problems. Each item is a different series-parallel topology of a single battery and resistors; the student reduces the network to an equivalent resistance and applies Ohm's law to find the total current. The topology (and hence the reduction sequence) changes between items, not only the numbers.

Problem 1 — (E) 2.00 A

Question. Determine the total current supplied by the battery of the circuit shown.

Reduce the circuit

1. R1 and R2 are in parallel: 1/R12 = 1/3.00 Ω + 1/6.00 Ω, so R12 = 2.00 Ω

2. R12 and R3 are in series: R123 = 2.00 Ω + 4.00 Ω = 6.00 Ω

Work back to the individual parts

1. The battery sees R123 = 6.00 Ω, so the total current is I = V / R = 12.00 V / 6.00 Ω = 2.00 A.

2. R12 and R3 are in series (R123), so each carries the same current 2.00 A; each voltage is I × R (0 V across a wire).

3. R1 and R2 are in parallel (R12), so each branch has the same voltage 4.00 V; each current is V / R (0 A through an open branch).

- 0.92 A: Student adds every resistor as if they were all in series.
